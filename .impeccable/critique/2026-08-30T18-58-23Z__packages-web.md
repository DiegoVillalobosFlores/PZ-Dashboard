## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored, not category-interchangeable. Corner brackets, opaque-slot-in-glass, Share Tech Mono readouts over a persistent map floor — no generic template produces this. Failures are the system violating its own stated rules.

Broken rules:
- One Signal Rule: wide Skills summary carries ~28 orange chips plus ~60 orange value spans in one block.
- Sharp Corner Rule / material system: the connection pill is the one capsule and the one overlapping element.

Deterministic scan: detect.mjs on packages/web — 0 findings, clean, with full HTML/CSS parsing (htmlparser2/css-select/css-tree/domutils installed into root node_modules; without them the detector runs regex fallback and undercounts). URL-mode scanning unavailable (requires puppeteer, not installed), so the deterministic layer covers source patterns only, not rendered contrast.

In-page measurement instead of overlays:
- Map street labels: 1.23:1 contrast (#c1c2c5 on cream map fill) across all 13 rendered labels. Home screen.
- One horizontally-overflowing scroll container: 524px content in a 350px box on compact.
- Sub-44px pointer targets: SPAN.pz-label at 20x14, 36x14, 54x14; SVG hit paths at 6x6 and 7x7.

Visual overlays: none. No injection attempted, no live-server started.

## Overall Impression

The visual world is not the problem. Five screens were designed and only two were composed. Home and compact Health hold their space; wide Health is a small figure in a large empty box, Skills is a data dump, Settings has a dead column, Inventory is a wall of identical cells. The screen labelled with a heart contains no health information.

Biggest opportunity: decide what each screen is for at a glance and delete the rest. Three of five screens currently require a focused reading session on a peripheral surface.

## What's Working

1. The persistent map floor pays off. Pan, zoom and marker survive all navigation. Panels open over a live world.
2. The bracket-and-slot depth system is legible and cheap: opaque box = slot, brackets = filled, warm black = in hand, readable with zero labels.
3. Compact Health is better composed than wide Health. The 5x2 grid under the figure is balanced with honest touch targets.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Compact vitals cluster hides 6 of 11 vitals behind a horizontal scrollbar
At 390x844 the cluster shows health, weight, thirst, fatigue, then a scroll track. Panic, pain, stress, sickness and the rest are off-screen behind a swipe nobody will discover.
Why: phone portrait is supported and the pill is the most glanceable element. The hidden ones are the alarm-state vitals.
Fix: drop the icon-only single row on compact. Show 4 primary vitals large; collapse the rest into a threshold-driven strip that renders a chip only when a value crosses amber or red.
Command: /impeccable adapt

### [P1] The Health screen contains no health
Shows a 3D figure and ten clothing slots. No body-part damage, wound, bandage, bleed, infection or moodle.
Why: checking health in PZ means checking injuries. A scratched player learns nothing and returns to the game's own panel — the behaviour the product exists to eliminate.
Fix: map body-part damage onto the figure already rendered, or rename the screen Equipment/Character.
Command: /impeccable shape

### [P1] Skills summary is an orange wall and breaks mid-word on compact
Wide: ~28 accented chips above the POSITIVE/NEGATIVE columns that restate the same facts. Compact: two ~150px columns wrapping mid-word — Athle/tic, Stron/g, Dextrou/s, Cowardl/y, Shor/t Sigh/ted, Wakef/ul. ~15 broken chips.
Why: mid-word breakage reads as a bug. The summary is the default compact tab, so it is a subscriber's first view of Skills.
Fix: remove word-break/overflow-wrap:anywhere from the trait-name column; default the compact tab to SKILLS; cut the summary to only effects not inferable from the traits list.
Command: /impeccable distill

### [P1] Map street labels are 1.23:1 contrast
#c1c2c5 on cream map fill, all 13 labels, on the Home screen.
Why: wayfinding is the map's job. The palette assumes a dark world; the map floor is light beige, so Ash Light is wrong the moment it lands on the map.
Fix: dedicated map-label treatment — dark ink with a light halo, or a translucent dark plate. Target 4.5:1 against the beige.
Command: /impeccable colorize

### [P2] Nothing in the item lists is named, and nothing is stacked
Eight icon-only container tabs (two near-identical key icons), unexplained green dot, unexplained orange hand glyph, unexplained green condition bar, 25 separate Egg Carton cells. Pane titled CONTAINERS while the container name sits below in secondary grey.
Why: this is the screen with the write actions, and it demands the most recall.
Fix: label the tabs, stack identical items with a mono count badge, invert the header hierarchy, add a legend or tooltips for dot / hand / bar.
Command: /impeccable clarify

### [P2] Every screen panel clips its own content under its bottom brackets
Wide Inventory cuts an item row mid-cell; wide Settings cuts the Pain row; compact Settings severs a sentence with the bracket drawn on top; compact Health runs a scroll track under its bottom bracket.
Why: a bracket means "this surface ends here." Content bleeding through reads as broken, with no signal that scrolling reveals more.
Fix: bottom padding clearing the bracket inset, plus a fade or fixed footer so a cut row reads as "more below."
Command: /impeccable layout

## Persona Red Flags

Alex (Impatient Power User): keys 1-5 and Escape work, and the nav number badges teach them without a tutorial. But no keyboard path into the item lists, so every move is mouse-only; no stacking means 25 interactions for 25 egg cartons; no dismiss affordance shown. Alex uses map and hotbar and never opens Inventory twice.

Sam (Accessibility-Dependent): fails on measurable grounds. Map labels 1.23:1. Interactive targets at 6x6 and 7x7px, pz-label click targets 14px tall against a stated handheld-touch need. Every item-list state is colour or unlabelled glyph with no text alternative. The horizontal-scroll container has no keyboard affordance.

Casey (Distracted Mobile User): the closest match to the stated user and the hardest failure. 5 of 11 vitals plus a scrollbar; Skills opens on orange fragments broken mid-word; Health after a bite shows a fashion doll. The map with the eased marker and the hotbar strip work.

Workshop Subscriber (project-specific): no first-run state anywhere. No empty state for "3 in range", no hint that 1-5 switch screens, no legend, no explanation of a blank category (the manifest distinguishes off / stale / never-received; the UI does not). "Mod disconnected" is the only system message and it overlaps whatever panel is open.

## Minor Observations

- The connection pill is the only capsule, the only overlapping element, and the only low-contrast alert. Dock it inside the vitals cluster.
- Empty slots and the empty hands widget render a generic 3D-cube glyph carrying no meaning; absence of brackets already reads as empty. The wide hands widget spends ~150px to say "Empty" twice.
- Wide Settings has a dead right column (~350x400px) between CHARACTER and CONDITIONS; reading order jumps left, right, left, full-width.
- Five Reset affordances plus Reset all are visible at once on wide Settings, all styled as text. Reset all is destructive and unconfirmed.
- Skill level bars are 10 segments of ~7px on wide, below the arm's-length legibility the brief demands. Majority-zero skills consume space equal to trained ones.
- DESIGN.md says right-edge map buttons are hidden in compact; the recenter button renders at 390px. One of the two is out of date.
- "SUMMARY 40" — bare number, no unit or label.
- /api/state returns 74KB per poll, dominated by fog cells.

## Questions to Consider

- What would the Health screen look like if it were only injuries, given the paperdoll lives one tap away in Inventory?
- The Skills summary has a Settings toggle. What does it mean that the design already suspects it is optional, and why is it still the default compact tab?
- If the player can read four things in a glance, which four? Every screen currently answers "all of them."
- Is the honest fix recolouring the map labels, or tinting the map so the dark palette you designed becomes true?
- What if connection state lived in the vitals cluster — as a dimming of the readout when data goes stale — instead of a pill floating over panels?
